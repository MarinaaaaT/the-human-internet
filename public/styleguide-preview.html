Do's and don'ts

Do

  • Let real photos bring all the color.
  • One weight. Hierarchy from size and gray.
  • Short, two-beat lines with periods.
  • Use the spiral to mean “verified.”

Don't

  • Colored buttons, gradients or new brand colors.
  • Bold, all-caps, or a second weight.
  • Checkmarks, shields or locks for “verified.”
  • Bounce, slide, or shadows on cards.
